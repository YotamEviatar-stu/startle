Airflow · stage S1 · open decision B3

The Repaired Peak

The sample-level gate is meant to be the first thing that notices an artifact. On this recording it notices nothing — and where it does notice, it erases the evidence the breath-level gate was going to judge.

What a “run” is

flag_excursions (airflow_qc.py:39) works one sample at a time: it marks every sample sitting more than 5×A away from a rolling median of the signal around it, where A is one breath-size. Marked samples that sit next to each other — merged across gaps under 0.2 s — form one run: a single contiguous stretch of bad signal, one artifact event.

QC_MAX_RUN_SEC = 1.0 (airflow_config.py:90) is a ceiling on how long a run may be, and it sorts runs into two branches: a run of 1 s or less is overwritten with a straight line drawn between the samples on either side of it; a run longer than 1 s is skipped and left completely alone. The stated logic is that a short glitch can be patched, a long one cannot.

What this does to the evidence

The same defect ran through the measurement I first used to argue for a sample-level gate. Asking “which extreme samples sit inside breaths the amplitude gate would keep?” against RA read from signal_clean counts every breath the sample gate has already flattened — the gate was being credited for hiding artifacts from the breath gate. Measuring RA on untouched signal_raw instead, against a 30 s local reference:

EV15/eve and MS13/mor, the two cases that made the strongest argument for keeping S1, leave the list entirely — measured on the recording, those breaths are above 5× the median and the breath-level gate takes them unaided. Of the three that remain, two sit at RA 4.36× and 4.97×, a hair under the cut, and the third (YR08/mor at 716.3 s, RP 18.0 s) is a lost-lock span rather than an amplitude case.

The trap in one line. A long artifact is never flagged, because the reference follows it. A short one is flagged and then flattened, so the breath it belongs to measures normal. Either way the breath survives — which is the outcome the whole cycle-rejection design exists to prevent.
