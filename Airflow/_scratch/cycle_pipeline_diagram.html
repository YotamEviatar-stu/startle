Rejection decided once, per breath cycle

Two figures. The first shows where the validity decision is made and who reads it. The second shows the same mechanism running on 50 seconds of real recorded airflow. Nothing in Airflow/ has been modified.

All numbers computed from the Layer-1 cache (24 subjects, 47 sessions, 20604 breath cycles). Thresholds are cohort percentiles taken condition-blind: kurtosis 12.10 = p99; the log-RA scale is frozen at 0.303 because per-session MAD varies 5.0x across recordings. Caveats: SUBJECTS_EXCLUDE was not applied, and the prototype detector omits despiking, so cycle counts will not match the shipped detect_cycles exactly. No beta or endpoint p-value is claimed here.
