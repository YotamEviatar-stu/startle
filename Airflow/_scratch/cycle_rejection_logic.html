Airflow · GLM path · rejection unit

Reject the breath, not the trial

A single bad breath currently survives every gate and lands in the regression target with full weight. In MS18/eve, four breaths — 3.3% of the session's samples — carry 61% of the session's total sum of squares in the RA series the GLM is fitted against.

What happens today

The pipeline has two ways to deal with an artifact, and neither removes it from the fit. despike_signal_raw linearly interpolates runs shorter than 1 s — the tip of the excursion — leaving its shoulders in place. Everything else is decided at the trial level: a trial is accepted or rejected as one boolean, and only a rejected trial's window is blanked to NaN in fit_series.

The gap between those two is where the artifact lives. A cough inside an accepted trial's window is never blanked, because nothing in the pipeline decides validity at the scale the artifact actually has: one breath.

The rule, in order

Six steps, all drawn on the same window and the same time axis as the figure above — OM16/mor 252–322 s — so each card can be read against the one before it. Validity is decided once, at the breath, and every later stage consumes that decision instead of re-deriving one.

S1

Find the invalid values

Condition-blind, on signal_raw. The reference has to be the session's own breath size, not a 1 s rolling median — a median that short rises with a multi-second artifact and hides it. Nothing is interpolated here; a flagged sample is evidence, not something to repair.

S2

Attach the verdict to a breath

Every sample belongs to exactly one cycle [onset, next_onset), so an invalid stretch condemns a whole breath. Cycle-level gates join here — amplitude against the session median, implausible period, no detectable inspiration inside the span.

S3

Close single-breath gaps

If cycle t−1 and cycle t+1 are both invalid, cycle t is invalid too. Inside a sensor event a lone passing breath is detector luck, not a measurement.

S4

Take the recovery breath with it

When the rejection is an amplitude extreme, the following cycle goes to NaN as well. Two independent arguments land on the same span: physiologically it is the signal sliding back from the artifact rather than a breath, and mechanically the bad cycle's RA is stored as a knot at the next onset — so its straight-line influence already covered both cycles.

S5

Admit trials by fraction

A trial is admitted when enough of the breaths overlapping its 12 s window survive. Three surviving breaths of four clears the floor; one of two does not — a boolean cannot tell them apart. A rejected trial loses its event in the design matrix and its window in the target.

S6

Solve on finite rows only

fit_pooled_session_glm already computes valid = isfinite(y) & isfinite(X) and solves over those rows, so a NaN enters neither β₀, nor β₁, nor the residual — while every surviving sample keeps its original timestamp.

Why the weighting matters

β is a least-squares quantity, so a sample's pull on it grows with the square of its excursion. In MS18/eve the four artifact breaths span 20.9 s out of 637.3 s of analysed signal — 3.3% of the samples — but 61.1% of the session's total sum of squares. In RP06/mor a single 9.5 s recovery cycle is 1.4% of the samples and 21.4% of the sum of squares. A session's pooled β is currently being set, in large part, by the breaths that are not breaths.

Still open

  • The sample gate cannot see these. flag_excursions at k=5 flags 0 runs in all of RP06/mor, because its reference is a 1 s rolling median that rises with the excursion. The working detector is the cycle-level amplitude ratio — that breath is 7.1× the session median.
  • "No oscillation" is not "low variance." RP06/mor 1341–1350 s reads as flat to the eye, but its rolling standard deviation is 1.48× the session median — a variance-based flat gate would miss it. It should be caught as no detected inspiration in a 9.5 s span instead.
  • An upper period bound is condition-sensitive. Evening breathes faster than Morning (median RP 3.30 s vs 3.60 s), so any fixed ceiling removes more Evening breaths by construction. Hardcode one for now, but report what it removes per session before trusting a β.
